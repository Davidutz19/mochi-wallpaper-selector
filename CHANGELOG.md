# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- A picker on the island: a strip of the wallpapers in a folder, the selected one big
  in the middle. Arrows, H and L, Home and End move; Enter sets; R sets a random one.
- Sets the wallpaper with awww, swww, hyprpaper or swaybg, whichever runs, or a
  command of your own.
- Remembers the last wallpaper and sets it again when Mochi starts.
- Actions for keys and scripts: `set`, `next`, `previous`, `random`, `restore`,
  `current`, `list`.
